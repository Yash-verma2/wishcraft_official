About WishCraft

Replacing generic birthday texts with gorgeous digital celebration websites.

Our Mission

In a world of fast messaging, standard "Happy Birthday!" texts get lost instantly. WishCraft was born from a simple idea: making it easy, free, and fast for anyone to design a highly personalized, premium website to celebrate their favorite people.

Whether it's for a partner, best friend, husband, or sibling, we help you package your thoughts, memories, photos, and music into a magical, private web address that you can send right at midnight.

What Makes Us Unique?

✨ Immersive Audio

Upload or select any song that plays automatically in the background to build the perfect romantic or celebratory mood.

📸 Memory Scrapbook

Upload up to 8 of your favorite photos, beautifully presented in an animated, touch-friendly carousel slideshow.

🎁 Interactive Virtual Gifts

Include a virtual surprise gift box that opens up with custom animations when clicked by the recipient.

🎨 Premium Typography & Layouts

Built using Outfit and Inter font pairings, Harmonious gradients, and gorgeous animations that look like a premium SaaS project.
