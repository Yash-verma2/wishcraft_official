What exactly is WishCraft?

WishCraft is where your most special moments come to life. Create elegant, personalized celebration pages for birthdays, anniversaries, and meaningful occasions—designed to transform simple wishes into unforgettable digital experiences.

Is it really free for everyone?

Yes! Our core celebration page generator is completely free. You can create, customize, and share as many pages as you want without any hidden charges.

Can I add my own music and photos?

Absolutely. You can upload multiple photos to create a gallery and either choose from our curated library or upload your own MP3 for the perfect soundtrack.

How do I share my creation?

Once you're done, we generate a unique, short link for your page. You can share it directly via WhatsApp, Instagram, or any other platform. It works perfectly on all devices.

Are the generated celebration pages private?

Yes, all generated pages are completely private. Only people who have the unique link you share can access the surprise page. We do not index your custom pages in public search engines.

Are my uploaded photos, videos, and personal files kept strictly private?

Yes, 100%. All photos, videos, audio tracks, and personal messages you upload are strictly confidential. We never share, stream, or upload your media to Instagram, YouTube, social media, or any other platform. Your private links are accessible only by the people you choose to share them with. Your privacy and data security are 100% guaranteed. 🔒
