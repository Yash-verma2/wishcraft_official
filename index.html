Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about creating the perfect celebration.

What exactly is WishCraft?

WishCraft is a premium digital celebration page builder. We help you create stunning, interactive, and personalized websites for birthdays, anniversaries, and special moments in just a few clicks.

Is it really free for everyone?

Yes! Our core celebration page generator is completely free. You can create, customize, and share as many pages as you want without any hidden charges.

Can I add my own music and photos?

Absolutely. You can upload multiple photos to create a gallery and either choose from our curated library or upload your own MP3 for the perfect soundtrack.

How do I share my creation?

Once you're done, we generate a unique, short link for your page. You can share it directly via WhatsApp, Instagram, or any other platform. It works perfectly on all devices.
